Philosophy

The Art of Handmade

100% Oven-Baked

We achieve a golden, light, and crispy texture in the oven, respecting the genuine flavor of each ingredient without excess grease.

Small Batches

We prepare each recipe daily in limited quantities to guarantee absolute freshness and oven warmth in every bite.

Clean Label

Premium flour, fine butter, local farm vegetables, and traditional Peruvian spices. Zero preservatives.

Andean Tradition & Culinary Excellence

Inspired by geometric perfection and respect for the earth of our ancestors, at Tummi every empanada is a unique piece shaped with the traditional handmade "repulgue" fold.

  • Hand-cut selected cuts of meat (lomo style).
  • Smoked and sautéed with natural Peruvian ají amarillo.
  • Vegetarian and 100% plant-based options of the highest quality.
